Mission

Communities experiencing crisis are not passive beneficiaries of aid.

White Impact Development Initiative was founded in 2025 and registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria in 2025 in response to the intersecting humanitarian, social, and governance challenges affecting underserved communities across Nigeria.

As a youth-led non-governmental organisation, we believe communities experiencing crisis are the active leaders of change. Our work strengthens access to education, protection services, safe civic spaces, and digital opportunity for young women, persons with disabilities, and displaced populations.

Education access Humanitarian education and child protection
Protection SRHR, safeguarding, and anti-GBV action
Digital opportunity AI literacy, creative work, and civic tech

The challenge

Barriers are interconnected, so our response is too.

The same communities that face gaps in schooling often face protection risks, limited digital access, and weak civic voice. We design programs that address those barriers together.

01

Education gaps

Children and young people still need safe access to learning, mentorship, and school support.

02

Protection risks

Communities need stronger safeguarding, SRHR, and anti-GBV systems that people can trust.

03

Digital exclusion

Young people deserve access to skills, tools, and opportunities that match a changing world.

04

Low civic participation

Transparent systems work better when young citizens can see, question, and help shape them.

What guides us

Our Core Values

Principles that shape every program, partnership, and decision we make.

Inclusion

We ensure underserved groups, especially young women and persons with disabilities, are actively involved in our programs and decision-making.

Integrity

We act with honesty, transparency, and ethical responsibility, building trust with communities, partners, and stakeholders.

Innovation

We embrace creative, forward thinking approaches leveraging technology and new ideas to solve complex social challenges.

Collaboration

We work with communities, partners, and institutions to strengthen impact through shared goals and collective action.

Sustainability

We design programs that create long-lasting change, building resilience and self-reliance in the communities we serve.

Equity

We are committed to fairness in access, opportunity, and outcomes ensuring no one is left behind because of background, gender, or ability.
