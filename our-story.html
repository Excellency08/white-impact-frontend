About WIDI

People. Planet. Progress.

A youth-led organisation turning local knowledge, shared opportunity, and practical action into lasting change.

WhiteImpact Development Initiative (WIDI) was established in 2025 with a simple belief: when people have access to knowledge, opportunities, and the right support, they can create meaningful change in their communities.

Across Nigeria, many young people and communities continue to face barriers to quality education, relevant skills, meaningful civic participation, digital opportunities, healthcare, environmental sustainability, and community development. We recognise that these challenges are interconnected and that addressing them requires more than short-term support.

As a youth-led and community-driven organisation, WIDI believes communities should not only receive support; they should be active participants, partners, and drivers of the solutions that shape their future. Our work begins with listening, understanding local priorities, building capacity, and developing practical solutions that respond to real needs.

We work across education and learning; youth leadership and civic participation; technology, innovation and digital transformation; environment, climate action and sustainability; health and well-being; and community development and humanitarian support. We bring together young people, communities, professionals, institutions, government, development partners, civil society organisations, researchers, and private-sector actors to share knowledge, develop skills, create opportunities, strengthen partnerships, and turn ideas into action.

Through our programmes and partnerships, WIDI creates spaces where young people can learn, connect, innovate, lead, participate, and contribute, while communities gain access to knowledge, skills, support, and practical opportunities that can improve lives and strengthen local development. We believe sustainable development begins with people. When people and communities are given the opportunity to learn, participate, lead, and contribute, they can unlock their potential and help build a more sustainable future.

FOUNDER

Sulaiman Shu'aibu Dattijo

Founder & Executive Director

Sulaiman Shu’aibu (Dattijo) is a Nigerian development practitioner, researcher, and public policy advocate with a focus on quality education, good governance, people-centred policy, digital innovation, youth development, and sustainable development.

He is the Founder and Executive Director of WhiteImpact Development Initiative (WIDI), where he works with young people and communities to promote quality education, digital inclusion, youth leadership and civic engagement, environmental sustainability, and community development.

Sulaiman is passionate about leadership, public policy, research, technology, and innovation, particularly in helping young people use AI and emerging technologies to learn, create, solve problems, and access new opportunities.

His leadership experience includes serving as the first Senate President of the Students’ Representatives Council at Kaduna State University, as well as leading youth and student initiatives at local and national levels. He has also participated in national and international initiatives focused on youth development, policy, education, science, and innovation.

His work is guided by integrity, service, humility, and evidence-based thinking. He believes that sustainable development begins with people and that young people should have the opportunity to learn, contribute, innovate, and help shape the future of their communities.
