About WIDI

People. Planet. Progress.

A youth-led organisation turning local knowledge, shared opportunity, and practical action into lasting change.

WhiteImpact Development Initiative (WIDI) was established in 2025 in response to persistent development gaps affecting young people, rural communities, and underserved populations across Nigeria.

Many communities continue to face barriers to quality education, relevant skills, healthcare, digital opportunities, environmental sustainability, and economic participation. We recognise that these challenges are interconnected, and that limited access to opportunity can restrict communities from fully contributing to their own development.

As a youth-led and community-driven organisation, WIDI believes communities should not only receive support; they should be active participants, partners, and drivers of the solutions that shape their future. Our work is centred on listening, understanding local priorities, and building practical solutions that create lasting change.

We bring together young people, communities, professionals, institutions, government, development partners, civil society organisations, researchers, and private-sector actors to create opportunities, share knowledge, develop skills, and turn ideas into action.

Through our programmes and partnerships, WIDI creates spaces where young people can learn, connect, innovate, lead, and contribute, while communities gain access to knowledge, support, and practical opportunities that improve lives.

FOUNDER

Sulaiman Shu'aibu Dattijo

Founder & Executive Director

Sulaiman Shu'aibu Dattijo is a Nigerian development practitioner, researcher, and public service advocate with a focus on energy economy and policy, digital innovation, youth development, governance, and sustainable development.

He is the Founder and Executive Director of WhiteImpact Development Initiative (WIDI) and is passionate about helping young people harness AI, digital tools, and emerging technologies for learning, creativity, and real-world impact.
